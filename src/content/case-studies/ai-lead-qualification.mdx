# AI Lead Qualification Automation

<Image src={cover} alt="AI Lead Qualification System" class="w-full rounded-lg shadow-lg my-8" />

## The Challenge

The client manually handled all incoming leads, causing slow response times, inconsistent qualification, and missed high-value opportunities. With no automated system in place, the team spent hours sorting through inquiries, often failing to prioritize the right prospects.

## Our Solution

We built an automated workflow using n8n and AI to capture, analyze, and route incoming leads in real time. The system evaluates each lead based on intent, urgency, and budget, then classifies them into Hot, Warm, or Cold categories for appropriate follow-up.

### How It Works

1. A user submits an inquiry through the website form
2. n8n captures the data via webhook
3. AI evaluates the lead based on intent, urgency, and budget
4. The lead is classified as Hot, Warm, or Cold
5. The lead is stored in the CRM
6. A notification is sent to the team
7. An auto-response is sent to the user

### Key Features

- **AI-Powered Scoring** - Leads are evaluated and classified automatically using intent, urgency, and budget signals
- **Instant Routing** - Hot leads go directly to the sales pipeline, warm leads receive follow-up, and cold leads enter a nurture sequence
- **CRM Integration** - All leads are stored and tracked in the client's existing CRM
- **Auto-Response** - Users receive an immediate acknowledgment, improving perceived responsiveness

## Results

- Reduced manual processing by **90 to 100%**
- Response time improved from **hours to seconds**
- Increased conversion through better prioritization

## Conclusion

By automating lead qualification with AI and n8n, we enabled instant response, better prioritization, and higher conversion rates. The client's team can now focus on closing deals instead of sorting through inquiries.
