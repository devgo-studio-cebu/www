# AI Customer Support Automation

<Image src={cover} alt="AI Customer Support Routing System" class="w-full rounded-lg shadow-lg my-8" />

## The Challenge

Support inquiries were manually categorized, leading to delays, incorrect routing, and inefficiencies in handling tickets. The team struggled to keep up with volume, and customers experienced long wait times before reaching the right department.

## Our Solution

We designed an automated system using n8n and AI that classifies and routes customer inquiries in real time. The AI identifies the category and urgency of each message, then routes it to the correct department instantly.

### How It Works

1. A customer submits a concern via email or contact form
2. n8n captures the message
3. AI identifies the category and urgency
4. The workflow routes the ticket to the correct department (Order Team, Finance Team, Tech Team, or Support Team)
5. A ticket is created in the system
6. The team is notified immediately

### Key Features

- **AI Classification** - Each message is analyzed for intent and urgency, then categorized automatically
- **Smart Routing** - Tickets are sent to the right department without manual intervention
- **Instant Ticket Creation** - Tickets are logged in the support system the moment they arrive
- **Team Notifications** - Relevant teams are alerted in real time so no ticket goes unnoticed

## Results

- Automated **100% of ticket categorization**
- Reduced routing time to **instant**
- Improved support efficiency by **60 to 80%**

## Conclusion

We automated customer support routing using AI, reducing response delays and improving operational efficiency. The client's support team now handles tickets faster and with greater accuracy.
