# RAG Product & Service Assistant

<Image src={cover} alt="RAG Product and Service Assistant" class="w-full rounded-lg shadow-lg my-8" />

## The Challenge

The client repeatedly answered the same questions about their services, pricing, and offerings. This led to inefficiencies and inconsistent responses, especially when multiple team members handled inquiries differently.

## Our Solution

We implemented a customer-facing AI assistant powered by RAG that retrieves accurate information from the client's structured business data. The assistant delivers instant, grounded responses to common questions without requiring human intervention.

### How It Works

1. A user asks a question about products or services
2. The system retrieves relevant business data from the knowledge base
3. AI generates a grounded response based on the retrieved information
4. The answer is delivered instantly to the user

### Key Features

- **Structured Knowledge Base** - Product and service data is organized and indexed for fast retrieval
- **Grounded Responses** - Every answer is based on actual business data, ensuring accuracy and consistency
- **Instant Delivery** - Customers receive answers in seconds without waiting for a human agent
- **Reduced Workload** - The team no longer needs to handle repetitive inquiries manually

## Results

- Automated up to **80% of repetitive inquiries**
- Improved response consistency across all channels
- Reduced manual workload significantly

## Conclusion

We implemented a RAG-based assistant to automate customer inquiries and ensure accurate, consistent responses. The client's team can now focus on complex tasks while the assistant handles the bulk of everyday questions.
