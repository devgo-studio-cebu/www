## The Real Cost of a Website in the Philippines

If you've ever searched "how much does a website cost in the Philippines," you've probably seen answers ranging from ₱2,000 to ₱500,000. That's not helpful. Let's break it down honestly so you can budget with confidence.

The truth is, website cost depends on three things: **what you need**, **who builds it**, and **what happens after launch**. We'll cover all three.

## Tier 1: Landing Pages (₱5,000 – ₱15,000)

A single-page website designed to capture leads or promote one product. Perfect for startups testing an idea or businesses that need a simple web presence fast.

- 1 page, mobile-responsive design
- Basic contact form or call-to-action
- Hosting setup and domain connection
- Typical turnaround: 3–5 business days

**Best for:** Startups, event pages, lead-generation campaigns

## Tier 2: Business Websites (₱25,000 – ₱80,000)

A multi-page website that establishes credibility and tells your brand story. This is what most SMEs need — a professional site that works as your 24/7 sales representative.

- 5–10 pages (Home, About, Services, Blog, Contact)
- SEO-optimized structure and meta tags
- CMS integration for easy content updates
- Typical turnaround: 2–4 weeks

**Best for:** Professional services, agencies, local businesses

## Tier 3: E-Commerce (₱80,000 – ₱200,000)

An online store with product listings, cart, checkout, and payment integration. If you're selling products online, this is your starting point.

- Product catalog with search and filters
- Payment gateway integration (GCash, Maya, bank transfer)
- Order management and inventory tracking
- Typical turnaround: 4–8 weeks

**Best for:** Retailers, wholesalers, D2C brands

## Tier 4: Custom Web Applications (₱200,000+)

Bespoke applications built for complex business logic — booking systems, CRMs, SaaS platforms, or workflow automation tools.

- Custom architecture and database design
- API integrations and third-party connections
- User authentication and role management
- Typical turnaround: 2–6 months

**Best for:** Enterprise, SaaS, businesses with unique operational needs

## What Affects the Price?

1. **Complexity** — More features = more development hours
2. **Design customisation** — Custom UI costs more than template-based design
3. **Content** — Who writes the copy? Who provides photos?
4. **Ongoing maintenance** — Hosting, updates, and security patches add up
