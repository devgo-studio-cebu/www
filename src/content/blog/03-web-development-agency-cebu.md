## Why Cebu for Web Development?

Cebu has emerged as the Philippines' second-largest tech hub — and in some ways, its most efficient. Here's why businesses choose Cebu-based agencies:

- **Lower operating costs** than Manila mean agencies pass savings to you
- **Strong talent pipeline** from universities like UP Cebu, CIT-U, and USJ-R
- **English fluency** across all technical and creative roles
- **Asia-Pacific time zone alignment** — ideal for Australian, Singaporean, and Japanese clients
- **Growing startup ecosystem** with co-working spaces like The Company Cebu and Spaces Cebu

The result: you get world-class web development at rates that make global agencies blush.

## What to Look for in a Web Development Agency

Not all agencies are equal. Before signing a contract, check these boxes:

1. **Portfolio depth** — Do they have case studies in your industry? Can they show live sites they've built?
2. **Technical transparency** — Do they explain their stack and why they chose it? Avoid agencies that hide behind jargon.
3. **Clear pricing** — Reputable agencies provide itemised quotes. If the price seems too good to be true, it probably is.
4. **Post-launch support** — Who handles updates, security patches, and hosting? This should be in the contract.
5. **SEO capabilities** — A website nobody finds is a waste of money. Ask about their SEO process.

## Services to Expect

A qualified Cebu web development agency should offer:

- **Custom website design and development** — Responsive, fast, and built for conversion
- **E-commerce solutions** — From Shopee-ready microsites to full WooCommerce/Shopify builds
- **SEO & performance optimisation** — Page speed, Core Web Vitals, and on-page SEO
- **CMS integration** — WordPress, Astro, or headless CMS depending on your needs
- **Ongoing maintenance** — Security updates, content changes, and performance monitoring
- **AI automation** — The best agencies now offer workflow automation as an add-on or standalone service

Typical pricing in Cebu (2026):
- Landing page: ₱8,000 – ₱25,000
- Business website (5–10 pages): ₱30,000 – ₱100,000
- E-commerce store: ₱80,000 – ₱200,000
- Custom web application: ₱200,000+
